# Feature ideas for TopForNow (prioritised, 19 Nov 2026)

Ranked by how much each one helps us reach the first ten paying customers, against how much work it is.

| # | Feature | Why it matters | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"You've been dethroned" email** (opt-in with the receipt email) | The replaced buyer is our hottest lead. One email with their time on top and a "take it back for $X" link. | S |
| 2 | **Dethroned + Verdict share cards** (templates 2 and 4 in `docs/brand/`) | Gives losers and free voters something to post. More shares, zero cost. | S |
| 3 | **Price-watch alerts** (email or Telegram when the spot changes or drops into your budget) | Free visitors get a reason to come back; buyers time their move. | M |
| 4 | **Leaderboard of reigns** (longest on top, most votes, best Agree %) | Status game for free users; drives return visits. | S |
| 5 | **Live mode on the homepage** (server-sent events: new post appears with a crown-drop animation, vote counts tick live) | Makes the spot feel like a live show; great for launch-day streams. | M |
| 6 | **Embeddable "I'm on TopForNow" badge** for buyers' sites/READMEs | Free backlinks from every buyer. | S |
| 7 | **Comment-free "responses"**: show the chain of paid replies as a thread | Turns arguments into revenue (people pay to answer an opinion). | S |
| 8 | **Owner payout tracker**: import Dodo payout CSV, mark payouts received | Real money view next to the estimate in the dashboard. | M |
| 9 | **Weekly recap page + RSS** ("this week: 6 rounds, $63, top verdict 71% agree") | Content for socials every week without writing it. | S |
| 10 | **Hindi / Hinglish UI toggle** | Bigger Indian audience, very low cost with server templates. | M |

Not recommended now: timed auctions, lotteries or anything with chance (Dodo bans games of chance), user accounts (not needed yet), AI moderation spend before there is volume.
