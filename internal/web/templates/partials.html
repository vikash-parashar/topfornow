How it works (30 seconds)
  1. There is one spot. Whatever is in it, everyone sees first.
  2. The very first post was free. After that, replacing the post costs $1, then $2, $4, $8, $16… Each replacement doubles.
  3. The buyer pays the full price shown. Their post stays on top until someone else pays the next price. No timer.
  4. Voting is free and never changes who is on top. Votes stay with the post forever.
  5. Replaced posts keep their own page, votes, link and results. Nothing is deleted.
